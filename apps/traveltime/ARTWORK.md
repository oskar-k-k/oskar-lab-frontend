# TravelTime artwork

The decorative Thailand cover was generated with the built-in Imagegen tool on 2026-11-25.
Runtime asset: `public/art/thailand-night.webp` (optimized WebP, locally served).
The original generation remains in the owner's generated-image library. No stock image,

The generic illustration is decorative and does not claim to depict a booked location.
Other station illustrations are locally authored SVG in `BookingVisual.tsx`.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative background image for the centered Thailand trip opening bubble in a minimal dark travel timeline web app. Create an exquisite cinematic editorial illustration of a Thailand tropical bay at blue hour, tall limestone karst silhouettes, a slender Thai longtail boat in calm luminous teal water, tiny warm amber lantern lights on a distant shore, delicate stars and a crescent moon. Rich midnight navy, deep petrol teal, restrained warm gold. Atmospheric, sophisticated travel journal, subtle painted texture, dreamy but beautifully natural, not cartoonish. Wide landscape composition, main scenery towards edges and lower half, calm dark uncluttered central area for white UI title overlay. Full bleed, no frame, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no people or identifiable private places. This is a generic destination illustration, not an actual booking location.

## Privacy and delivery

Generation used only the generic destination/art direction above, with no account details,
bookings, addresses, documents or private images. Browsers load the optimized asset from
the app's own origin; no image generation service is contacted at runtime. Existing hosting
access-log retention applies. No new user uploads, stored personal data or permissions.

The 2026-11-27 sketchbook redesign reuses this cover as a postcard. Paper grain and tape
are CSS; station stickers use the existing local SVG illustrations recolored in sage and ink.
No imagery from the design-reference screenshot is copied or downloaded.
